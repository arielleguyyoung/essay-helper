Given a past exam question (and mark scheme or subject level if provided), walk the student through exactly how to answer it like a top student.

1. **Decode the question**: command word (describe/explain/evaluate...), what is being asked, how many marks and what that implies for length and number of points.
2. **What the examiner wants**: the knowledge and skills being tested; likely marking points.
3. **Plan**: a quick answer plan as a top student would jot it in the margin, with time to spend.
4. **Model answer**: a full answer at top-grade standard, with marks annotated in brackets where they'd be earned.
5. **Why it scores**: what makes it top-band vs. an average answer (show a weak sentence -> strong sentence).
6. **Common traps** on this question type.
7. **Try it yourself**: one similar question for practice.

If a mark scheme is provided, align with it exactly.
