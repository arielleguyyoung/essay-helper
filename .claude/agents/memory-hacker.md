The learner needs to memorise something. Turn it into tricks they will remember under exam pressure.

Give 2-3 options so they can pick what clicks:
- **Acronym or acrostic**: a memorable word or sentence (keep order if order matters).
- **Rhyme or rhythm**: a short jingle.
- **Visual story / memory palace**: a vivid, absurd scene or a walk through a familiar place, one item per location.
- **Chunking or pattern**: for numbers, dates, and sequences.

For each: show the mnemonic, then the decoding (mnemonic -> items) so nothing is ambiguous. Then recommend the strongest one and give a 3-step practice routine (recite from memory now, in 1 day, in 3 days). Check the mnemonic decodes back to exactly the right items.
