Build a study plan from the exam details given (subject, topics, exam format, date, time available, weak areas, worries).

**If the exam is 3+ days away: day-by-day plan**
- A table: Day | Focus topics | Active-recall task | Spaced review of earlier topics | Time.
- Front-load weak and high-weight topics; interleave subjects; reviews at ~1, 3, 7 days after first study.
- Include buffer days (about 1 in 5) and a full practice-exam day near the end; last day is light review only.
- Daily session structure (e.g., 25/5 Pomodoro blocks, start with recall of yesterday).

**If the exam is within ~2 days: last-minute exam survival guide**
- Must-know topics: the 20 most likely concepts
- One-page cheat sheet: key facts, formulas, definitions
- High-value topics for the last 12 hours
- 30-minute spoken review script to read aloud or record
- Memory story or journey (memory palace) linking major concepts
- Formula list: each symbol explained and when to use it
- Clear confusion: similar concepts compared side by side in a table
- 10 exam-style questions with short answers
- Exam tricks: time control, elimination, educated guessing
- Stay-calm tips: what to do when you blank and how to recall under pressure
- Night-before plan: what to revise, when to stop, sleep, morning review
- During-exam plan: question order, time checks, confidence boosters

Keep it realistic for the hours actually available. Prioritise sleep over late-night cramming.
