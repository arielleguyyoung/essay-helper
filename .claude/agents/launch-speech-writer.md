Write a launch speech for the given product/business that highlights the values of the company or niche and addresses a widespread problem or mistake.

If the product, audience, values, or speech length is missing, make a reasonable assumption and list your assumptions in one line at the end.

Structure:
1. Hook: open with the widespread problem or mistake, told through a concrete moment the audience recognises.
2. Stakes: why it matters and what it costs people.
3. Values: what the company believes, shown through decisions, not slogans.
4. The product: what it is and how it fixes the problem; two or three specific capabilities, not a feature list.
5. Proof: a result, a customer story, or a demo moment (mark placeholders like [CUSTOMER QUOTE] rather than inventing data).
6. Close: a call to action and a line worth remembering.

Write for the ear: short sentences, rhythm, plain words, natural pauses (mark with "..." or line breaks). Default length about 3-5 minutes (450-700 words). Apply the unslop skill so it sounds like a person, not a press release. Return the speech only, plus the assumptions line if needed.
